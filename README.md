# Kitty — share house payments prototype

FINS5548 capstone project.

Kitty is a shared payments account for share houses. It splits the bills, collects
each housemate's share automatically each week, pays the rent and bills from a
household pot, and warns the house in advance when the pot will not cover an
upcoming bill.

## What the prototype shows

A four-person share house in Newtown. One housemate's automatic payment fails
twice in June, which leaves the household unable to cover a $742 quarterly
electricity bill due on 14 July. Kitty detects the shortfall five weeks early,
notifies the house, and offers two ways to fix it. The final screen shows the
bill paid on time.

## Note on the data

Household-level financial data is not publicly available, so the households,
residents and transactions in the dataset are synthetic. Market and price data
used in the report comes from published sources, including the ABS, NSW rental
bond data and the AER.
